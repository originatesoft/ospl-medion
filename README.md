**OSPL Medion** is a purpose-built ecommerce platform for pharmacies, medical stores, and healthcare retailers that need a compliant, reliable, and fully operational online presence. Developed by **Originate Soft Pvt Ltd**, it covers everything a pharmacy business needs to sell online — prescription management, medicine catalogue, inventory control, order fulfilment, and customer health records — in one integrated system built specifically for how pharmacy retail works.

---

## Why OSPL Medion

Selling medicines online is not the same as selling clothes or electronics. Prescription verification, expiry date tracking, substitute medicine suggestions, scheduled drug restrictions, and GST-compliant invoicing are not optional features — they are baseline requirements. A generic ecommerce platform will never handle these out of the box, and patching them in later is expensive and unreliable.

OSPL Medion was built from the ground up for pharmacy and healthcare retail — not adapted from a horizontal ecommerce template — so every feature is designed with compliance, accuracy, and patient safety in mind.

## Core Features

### Medicine Catalogue & Product Management
- Structured medicine listing with generic name, brand name, manufacturer, composition, and dosage
- Prescription-required (Rx) and over-the-counter (OTC) product classification
- Substitute and alternative medicine suggestions per product
- Expiry date tracking and automated low-stock / near-expiry alerts
- Bulk product upload with batch and batch number support
- Category management: tablets, syrups, injections, surgical, wellness, personal care, and more

### Prescription Management
- Upload prescription at checkout — image or PDF
- Pharmacist review and approval workflow before order processing
- Prescription validity tracking and reorder reminders
- Digital prescription storage per customer account

### Storefront & Customer Experience
- Mobile-first, responsive storefront with fast search by medicine name, salt, or brand
- Chronic medicine quick-reorder for repeat customers
- Health categories: diabetes, cardiac, ortho, dermatology, paediatrics, and more
- Dosage information, usage instructions, and side-effect details per product
- Wishlist and save-for-later for regular medicines

### Order & Fulfilment Management
- Order dashboard with prescription verification status tracking
- Partial order fulfilment for out-of-stock items
- Cold-chain delivery flag for temperature-sensitive medicines
- Return and refund workflow with pharmacist-controlled approval
- Shipping integrations with major logistics and hyperlocal delivery partners

### Customer Accounts & Health Records
- Registered customer profiles with order and prescription history
- Family member profiles under one account
- Medication reminders and refill alerts
- Loyalty points and repeat-order discounts
- Doctor and hospital tie-up module for institutional orders

### Payments & Billing
- Multi-gateway payment integration (cards, UPI, wallets, net banking, COD)
- GST-compliant invoice generation with HSN code support
- Insurance claim documentation support
- Guest checkout for one-time purchases

### Admin & Inventory Management
- Pharmacist and admin dashboard with role-based access
- Real-time inventory tracking with batch-level expiry management
- Scheduled drugs and controlled substance flagging
- Purchase order and supplier management module
- Sales and stock reports with low-margin and high-demand alerts

### Marketing & Growth Tools
- SEO-ready medicine and category pages with editable meta fields
- Abandoned cart recovery automation
- Health blog and content module for organic traffic
- Promotional banners for seasonal health drives and wellness campaigns
- Referral programme and coupon management
